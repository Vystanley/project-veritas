# Disclaimer

Veritas is an experimental AI-powered tool that analyzes short-form social media videos. It downloads the video, transcribes the audio, reads on-screen text, runs web searches, and uses AI language models to assess whether the claims in the video are supported by publicly available sources.

**Please read this before relying on any result.**

## Veritas is not professional fact-checking

Veritas verdicts are generated automatically by AI. They are not reviewed by human journalists, editors, or subject-matter experts before being displayed. Veritas is not a member of the International Fact-Checking Network (IFCN) and does not follow any certified fact-checking methodology. Use Veritas as a starting point for further research, not as a final authority.

## Verdicts can be wrong

AI models make mistakes. Verdicts may be incorrect due to:

- Outdated or incomplete web search results
- Misinterpreted transcripts or on-screen text
- Limited context (a short video cannot always capture nuance)
- Biases or errors in the underlying AI models
- Rate limits or temporary failures in data providers

A "False" or "Mostly False" verdict does not prove that the creator of the video lied or acted in bad faith. A "True" verdict does not mean every detail is accurate. Always verify important claims against primary sources before sharing, citing, or acting on them.

## Not a source of legal, medical, financial, or safety advice

Never rely on Veritas for decisions affecting your health, finances, legal status, safety, or the safety of others. Consult qualified professionals for those decisions.

## Claims about real people

Veritas may display verdicts that involve claims about named individuals, including public figures. These verdicts are automated assessments, not accusations. If you are the subject of a Veritas verdict and believe it is inaccurate or unfair, you may request a review or removal — see the correction process in the Terms of Use.

## Source links

Veritas links to third-party websites (news outlets, Wikipedia, etc.). We do not control those sites and are not responsible for their content, accuracy, or availability.

## Experimental software

Veritas is a student portfolio project and is provided "as is," without warranty of any kind. Features may change, break, or disappear without notice.
